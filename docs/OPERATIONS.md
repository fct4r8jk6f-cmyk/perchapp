# Perch — Remote Operations Runbook (running it on a 9-5)

*How to operate Perch Thursdays **entirely online**, async, in ~45 min/week of evening time — without taking a day off or working the event in person. Companion to [BOOTSTRAP.md](BOOTSTRAP.md) (the zero-budget launch plan); this is the operational adaptation of its **Phase A loop** for a founder who works a day job.*

> ⚠️ **Not legal advice.** One assumption below — that the operator arranges but does **not attend** events — weakens a liability mitigation in [BOOTSTRAP.md](BOOTSTRAP.md) (which assumed you'd attend and vet in person). Flag this to counsel; see [the safety section](#safety-when-youre-not-in-the-room) and [COUNSEL_BRIEF.md](COUNSEL_BRIEF.md).

---

## The one thing that doesn't change

Perch is an **in-person** product — that's the whole thesis ("doing something real"). Attendees meet face to face; you can't move *that* online without becoming a weaker product. What goes fully online is **your job as the operator.** Two facts make this work:

1. **Events are Thursday evenings (7 PM)** — already outside your 9-5.
2. **The app already does the coordination** — group chat, day-of status, check-in, and the post-event survey are all built. Your weekly work is recruiting, forming the group, locking a venue, and reading the numbers — all of which are async and laptop-only.

**Two hard rules for everything below:** no daytime tasks (the venue takes walk-ins, so no business-hours phone calls), and no in-person presence required (an *anchor attendee* is your eyes and ears — see below).

---

## The weekly cadence (anchored on Thursday 7 PM)

Everything is timed relative to the event. All of it is evening/laptop work.

| When | Task | Time | Done in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sun/Mon eve** | **Open & recruit.** Confirm next Thursday's group is open. Post the quiz/demo link in 2–3 free channels (see [Recruiting](#recruiting-channels-all-free-all-online)). | ~15 min | Phone/laptop |
| **Tue eve** | **Lock the venue.** Pick a walk-in-friendly spot (no booking). Send a heads-up DM/email so they expect ~6 people. Set the venue in the group. | ~10 min | Laptop |
| **Wed eve** | **Form the group.** Check sign-ups; confirm 4–6 matched with a balanced archetype mix; nudge the waitlist if under 4. Confirmations send automatically. | ~10 min | Laptop |
| **Thu daytime** | **Nothing.** The app sends the reminder. You're at work. | 0 | — |
| **Thu 7 PM** | **The event.** You don't attend. The app coordinates check-in; your anchor attendee runs point in the room. | 0 | — |
| **Thu night / Fri** | **Measure.** Read check-in count (= show-up rate), read the post-event survey, DM the anchor for a 2-line debrief. Log the numbers. | ~10 min | Phone/laptop |
| **Weekend** | **Moderate & reflect.** Handle any reports/support (async, in-app). Tweak the format. Decide next week's date/venue. | variable | Laptop |

**≈45 min/week, zero daytime, zero in-person.** If a week is busy, the non-negotiables are: group is formed by Wed night, and you read the numbers by Friday.

## Recruiting channels (all free, all online)

Post the **quiz/demo link** — the quiz *is* the landing page and the share hook:

- r/montreal and r/montreal-area university subs
- McGill / Concordia / UdeM Discord & Facebook groups
- Bumble BFF, Meetup.com (free), local Slack/Discord communities
- Friends-of-friends reshares of the quiz result

Aim to get ~8–10 sign-ups to reliably seat 4–6. Track which channel each person came from in your weekly log — you'll want to know what actually works.

## The anchor attendee (your eyes and ears)

Since you're not in the room, **recruit one reliable person per event** to be your anchor. Ideally someone you trust from a previous Thursday (this is why reliability compounds). Their job, in exchange for a free/comped hang or just goodwill:

- Confirm the group actually found each other and the venue worked.
- Send you a 2-line debrief that night: *did everyone show? did it feel good? anything break?*
- Be the human fallback if someone's stuck outside / can't find the table.

This is the single mitigation that replaces "the founder is there." It's not as good as attending — but it makes remote operation viable and gives you qualitative signal the survey can't.

## What to measure (the only numbers that matter)

Keep a dead-simple weekly log (a spreadsheet row per Thursday):

| Date | Signed up | Seated | **Showed up** | **Show-up %** | Would-come-again | Friend-adds | Recruit channel | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

The two that decide everything (from [PLAN.md](PLAN.md)):
1. **Show-up rate ≥ ~85%** — pulled straight from in-app check-ins.
2. **Would-come-again / friend-adds** — from the post-event survey + the anchor debrief.

**Success bar before scaling spend (unchanged from BOOTSTRAP Phase A):** show-up ≥ ~80% and most people say they'd return. If not, change the *format*, not the code.

## Safety when you're not in the room

A stranger-meetup app where the operator is absent needs its safety posture stated, not assumed:

- **In-app tools do the work:** first-names-only before friendship, private blocking, report → 24h review, take-a-break. These don't require you on site.
- **Emergencies are not a moderation queue.** Make sure the in-app safety/help copy points to **911 and local crisis lines** directly — a report reviewed within 24h is the wrong channel for an in-the-moment emergency. Verify this is wired before the first stranger event.
- **Your anchor is the human escalation path** for the night-of: they can call you, and call 911 if needed.
- **Liability flag for counsel:** BOOTSTRAP's "run your own hosted events (you vet and attend)" mitigation assumed you're physically present. Operating remotely changes that. Ask counsel whether arranging-but-not-attending changes your exposure, and whether the venue choice / waiver copy needs to reflect it. This is one of the things [COUNSEL_BRIEF.md](COUNSEL_BRIEF.md) should resolve over email/video.

## Walk-in venue criteria (so there's never a daytime call)

Pick venues that need **no booking, no deposit, no business-hours coordination**:

- Takes walk-ins and can absorb a table of ~6 on a weeknight (cafés, board-game bars, casual breweries).
- Comfortable for 2+ hours without pressure to leave.
- Easy to find / describe in one line (the app shows the venue text; attendees self-navigate).
- A heads-up DM/email the night before is courtesy, not a reservation — keeps it zero-friction and async.

Rotate 2–3 known-good venues so you're never scrambling.

## What to automate next (optional, removes more weekly work)

The app coordinates *attendees*; almost nothing automates the *operator*. If you want the weekly loop to shrink further, the high-value targets (build later, not blockers):

- **Batch matching** for the Thursday group, so "form the group" becomes a review-and-approve instead of manual mixing.
- **A weekly operator digest** (sign-ups, who's matched, show-up from last week) so the numbers come to you.
- **Auto-open** next week's group + auto-post the recruit reminder.

None of these are needed to start — the cadence above runs on the product as it exists today.
