# SOCIAL.md — Perch social content kit

Source of truth for Perch's organic social content (Instagram-first: **@perchappcanada**, 32 followers as of 2026-06-21).

> **Handle note:** the live IG account is **@perchappcanada** (tracks the non-geo "PerchApp" branding decision; `@perchapp` was apparently taken). Keep socials, app-store name, and domain consistent with this before any paid push. `#PerchMtl` is kept below only as a local *discovery* hashtag for the Montreal pilot, not as the brand handle.

Everything here is bilingual **en / fr-CA** to match the app. The four Reel concepts are built around **Higgsfield's** camera-control strengths (cinematic moves, ambiance, B-roll, text-in-image) and deliberately use **no synthetic people / fake testimonials** — Perch sells authentic in-person connection, so fake AI "members" would undercut the brand. Use AI for ambiance, B-roll, motion graphics, and posters only.

---

## Shared production settings (every shot)

- **Format:** 9:16 vertical · **Stills:** Nano Banana Pro → animate via image-to-video (Kling 3.0 = cheapest credits; reserve Veo 3.1 for hero shots) · **Duration:** 3–4s per shot
- **Look (paste into every prompt):** `warm golden-hour palette, shallow depth of field, 35mm film look, soft film grain, cozy inviting mood`
- **Faces rule:** hands, silhouettes, mugs, bokeh — **never a recognizable face**. Protects the trust brand and cuts credit cost.
- **Text:** bake bilingual hero text in **post (CapCut/Canva)**, not in Higgsfield — cleaner typography, free.
- **Free stack baseline:** Canva (free) + CapCut (free) + Meta Business Suite (free). Higgsfield is a paid power-up — watch credit burn.

## 2. "The opposite of the scroll" — hero / best camera trick

**Higgsfield prompts**
```
Shot 1 — POV vertical shot, a hand holding a smartphone in a dark room, cold blue
screen light, endless blurred social feed, lonely moody atmosphere.
Camera: subtle handheld FPV drift. 9:16

Shot 2 (HERO — use Veo 3.1) — Cinematic vertical transition: a glowing smartphone
screen morphs into a warm real café table, cold blue light turning into golden
warm light, heavy motion blur. Camera: fast Whip Pan into 360 Orbit transition. 9:16

Shot 3 — Cinematic vertical shot orbiting a small round café table with five drinks
and resting hands (NO faces), warm candlelight, laughter implied by motion.
Camera: smooth 360 Orbit. [+ shared look] 9:16
```
> Shot 2 is the credit-worthy one. If the morph fights you, generate the cold "phone" still and warm "table" still separately and let the Whip Pan blur bridge them.
